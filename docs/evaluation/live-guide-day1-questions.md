# Live guide: Day 1 test plan (50 questions)

Expected outcomes recorded before submitting this batch. Prompts are agent-authored, with fresh wording over a mix of familiar and new scenarios; this is not an independent holdout. Expected evidence is based on the indexed corpus. No personal details.

A pass needs a direct, source-supported answer that covers the requested details, or an explicit decline with no irrelevant cards for fully unsupported requests. A partial result can retain relevant evidence without providing a usable answer. For mixed requests, assess each part; declining the unsupported part alone is not enough.
