## Method and grading

The exact same 50 prompt strings were verified against both earlier captures and submitted once each in the same order through the public Search interface. First completed results were retained, including all visible source cards and their URLs. Q8 had a browser Search-button wait timeout; the completed first answer was then read without resubmission. That capture issue is not counted as an app answer failure. No favourable answer retry was selected.

The original legal checkpoints and rubric were retained: Pass = the core request is correctly answered with evidence; Partial = meaningful requested content is present but another required element, accuracy or completion is missing; Fail = the core request is not answered. Minor precision caveats are documented, as in earlier runs. The owner originally supplied topic summaries and counts rather than the exact full curated record bodies, so fine-detail misses do not establish that the app promised every legal exception in its initial scope.

Retrieval adequacy: **46 adequate and 4 partial**, with no wholly missing core evidence under this classification. Q50 is partial because two required category records are present and the third is absent. This assesses the visible cards, not the contents of an internal index.

Citation/card support: **49 supported and one partly supported (Q14)**. Every answer used source labels that resolved to displayed evidence. This is completed-claim support against curated cards, not a substitute for independently checking every card against the complete official instrument.

Across the run, **36 distinct displayed labels** appeared: 13 Act, 10 Rules, 7 advertisement-guideline and 6 dark-pattern labels. The label mix differs from the original declared 15/10/5/6 split. These are display labels, not internal record IDs, so the matching total of 36 does not certify an inventory match.
